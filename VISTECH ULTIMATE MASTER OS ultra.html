    EMOTIONS ARE DATA — NOT DIRECTIONS
    🧊
    THE 24-HOUR RULE
    Gussa, dard, excitement, fear — koi bhi emotion ho — 24 ghante koi bada decision nahi. Soo jao, kal soche. 80% "urgent" problems khud solve ho jaati hain.
    RULE: Agar aaj decide karna bahut zaroori lag raha hai — it's not. Wait 24 hours.
    🎯
    COMPARISON IS POISON
    Accenture wala, GF ka naya partner, dost ki job — ye sab alag journey hain. Tu apne yesterday se compare kar. Unka path tera path nahi hai. Kabhi nahi tha.
    RULE: Roz 1 cheez likho — "Aaj main kal se better hun kyunki..."
    🔇
    VALIDATION SEEKERS FAIL
    Kisi ko prove karne ki zaroorat nahi. Na family, na ex, na dost. Jab prove karna band karega — tab teri asli power shuru hogi. Apne liye karo. Sirf apne liye.
