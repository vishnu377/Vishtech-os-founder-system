Founder Rules

Ye tod ke clients ja chuke hain — ye follow karo aur stable raho

10 Golden Rules — Vishnu Founder OS
R1
QR system pehle — sab baad mein
Cab, dry fruits, clothing — sab baad mein aayega. Abhi sirf QR. Focus split kiya toh dono fail honge. EK kaam, poori energy.
R2
Haan bol chuke 10 logon ko aaj close karo
Ye pehle se warm leads hain. Inka intezaar mat karo — unhe call karo, installation date do. Ek call = potential ₹24,000/year.
R3
Written agreement — har baar, har client
Simple WhatsApp pe bhi chalega: "Ye tera rate hai, ye teri service hai, agle mahine renew hogi." Screenshot = agreement. No exceptions.
R4
Numbers track karo — feeling pe nahi chalte
Demos, closes, revenue — har din likho. Jo measure nahi hota wo improve nahi hota. Notebook ya phone notes — kuch bhi, but daily.
R5
Har month ke 25 tarikh ko renewal conversation shuru karo
30 ko yaad karna mat — 5 din pehle results bhejo, payment link bhejo. Client khud anticipate karega.
R6
Ghar waalon ko monthly update do
Kitne clients, kitna income, agla mahine ka target. Unhe loop mein rakkho — unkaa doubt dhire dhire khatam hoga. Transparency = trust.
R7
Planning se zyada execution
13 pages ke plans bane. Ab sirf ek cheez chahiye — QR system live karo aur pehla client close karo. Aaj. Right now.
R8
Ek baar mein ek business — jab stable ho tabhi agle mein jao
QR se ₹50K/month stable hone tak — cab, dry fruits, clothing pe time mat lagao. Ye rule save karega. Seriously.
R9
Bholebaba pe trust — kaam pe focus
Mandir har subah. Phir ghar se niklo. Mehnat aur bhakti saath chalti hai. Teri strength yahan hai.
R10
24 saal ki age mein ₹1L/month — ye possible hai, tu kar sakta hai
10 logon ne HAAN kaha. Market ne validate kiya. System ready ho raha hai. Ek kadam aage. Sirf ek.
