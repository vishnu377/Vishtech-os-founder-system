Founder Rules

Ye tod ke clients ja chuke hain — ye follow karo aur stable raho

⚡ 10 Golden Rules
R1
QR system pehle — sab baad mein
Cab, dry fruits, clothing — sab baad. Abhi sirf QR. Focus split = dono fail.
R2
10 "haan" waalon ko aaj close karo
Warm leads hain. System ready hone pe turant call karo — installation date do.
R3
Written agreement — har baar, har client
Simple WhatsApp message bhi chalega. Screenshot = agreement. No exceptions.
R4
Numbers track karo — feeling pe nahi chalte
Demos, closes, revenue — har din likho. Jo measure nahi hota wo improve nahi hota.
R5
Har month 25 tarikh ko renewal conversation shuru karo
Results bhejo, payment link bhejo. 30 ko yaad nahi karna — 5 din pehle karo.
R6
Ghar waalon ko monthly update do
Clients, income, agla target — unhe loop mein rakkho. Doubt dhire khatam hoga.
R7
Planning se zyada execution
Plans already ready hain. Ab ek cheez — QR live karo. Aaj. Right now.
R8
Ek baar mein ek business — jab stable ho tabhi agle mein
QR se ₹50K/month stable hone tak — doosra kuch mat karo. Seriously.
R9
Bholebaba pe trust — kaam pe focus
Mandir har subah. Phir niklo. Mehnat aur bhakti saath chalti hai.
R10
24 saal — ₹1L/month possible hai, tu kar sakta hai
10 logon ne HAAN kaha. Market ne validate kiya. Sirf ek kadam aage.
