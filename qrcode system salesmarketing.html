Minute 2:30-3:00 — Close (Numbers dikhao)
"Cost hai sirf ₹2,000/month. Ek din mein recover hoti hai. Kya aaj start karein? Kal tak QR ready ho jaayega."

Objections — Complete Answers

Har "nahi" ke peeche ek reason hota hai — ye reason handle karo

"Mujhe nahi chahiye / sab theek chal raha hai"
Bhaiya, main bhi yahi sochta tha agar main aapki jagah hota. Lekin ek calculation karna chahta hoon — Zomato pe month mein kitna commission jaata hai? [Get number.] Ye sirf ₹2,000 mein recover hota hai. Kyunki "theek chal raha hai" ka matlab ye nahi ki aur better nahi ho sakta.
"Technology nahi samjhi / staff nahi seekhega"
Isiliye main khud install karta hoon aur free training deta hoon — 30 minutes mein aapka poora staff sikhega. Ye QR code hai — customer scan karta hai, aapko WhatsApp milta hai. Aur kuch nahi. Jo WhatsApp use karte hain wo ye kar sakte hain.
"₹2,000 zyada hai / budget nahi"
Bhaiya, seedha puchta hoon — ek din mein Zomato commission kitna jaata hai? [₹500-1000 usually.] Matlab 2-4 din mein recover hota hai ₹2,000. Aur baaki 26 din? Pure profit. Lekin agar ab tight hai toh pehle mahina ₹0 mein de raha hoon — result dekho, phir decide karo.
"Pehle try karke dekhna chahta hoon"
Perfect — isiliye 1 mahina FREE de raha hoon. Koi commitment nahi, koi contract nahi. Setup aaj, result 2 weeks mein. Pasand na aaye toh QR uthar dunga. Fair hai?
"Customers QR scan nahi karte"
Ye 2022 ki concern thi — 2024 mein 80%+ India smartphone use karta hai. Aur QR scanning habit pandemic ke baad bahut zyada badhi hai. Lekin proof chahiye? Ek hafta free lagao — khud dekho scan rate. Average 35-40% customers scan karte hain pehle hafte mein.
"Internet band ho jaaye toh kya?"
Menu offline bhi kaam karta hai — phone mein cached hota hai. Sirf loyalty points update ke liye internet chahiye, wo bhi baad mein sync hota hai. Internet dependent features: order + review. Ye tab bhi manual backup hota hai. 99% time internet hota hai — is 1% ke liye poora system band nahi karte.
"Doosra company sasta deti hai"
Zaroor — ₹500/month wala system bhi market mein hai. Lekin wo sirf QR deta hai — loyalty + WhatsApp order + review collection + customer data + analytics + hamare jaise support nahi deta. Ek comparison karein features ka? Aur hamare saath Jaipur mein local support hai — kuch gadbad hua toh main khud aata hoon.
"Zomato chhod nahi sakta — orders wahan se aate hain"
Bilkul mat chhodna — wo parallel chalata raho. Ye QR un customers ke liye hai jo pehle se aapke yahan aate hain ya direct aate hain. Naye customers Zomato se — existing customers direct. Dono channels side by side. Ek month mein dekho — direct orders kitne hue, commission kitna bacha.
"Kya ye legal hai? Data security?"
100% legal — ye simple web application hai, same technology jo Domino's aur McDonald's use karta hai. Customer data sirf aapka — koi third party access nahi. HTTPS secured. Main agreement mein likhhta hoon specifically. Aur honestly — kirana mein jo paper khata hai, usme bhi data tha — ye actually zyada secure hai.
"Abhi busy season hai, baad mein karenge"
Bhaiya, busy season mein hi sabse zyada fayda hota hai — jab 500 customers aa rahe hain toh 200 scan karenge aur loyal ho jaayenge. Off-season mein toh customers hi nahi hote scan karne ke liye. Busy season mein setup = maximum ROI. Kya kal subah 9 baje se pehle install kar doon jab rush nahi hota?

Sales Psychology — QR ke liye

Local business owner kaise sochta hai — aur kaise convince karo

Loss Aversion
Bachane ki baat karo, kamane ki nahi
Local owner loss se 3x zyada react karta hai gain se. Unka hard-earned money jaata dikhao.
Wrong: "Revenue 20% badhega"
Right: "Har mahine ₹18,000 ja raha hai Zomato ko. Aap mujhe ₹2,000 do, wo ₹18K bachao."
Neighbor Effect
Paas waali dukaan ne lagaaya
Local business owners ek dusre ko dekh ke decide karte hain. Competitor ka naam powerful trigger hai.
"[Street pe same dukaan] ne last month lagaaya — roz unhe 8-10 direct WhatsApp orders aa rahe hain. Unse pooch sakte ho — main number deta hoon."
Zero Risk Anchor
Pehla mahina free
Local owner risk-averse hai. "Free" word removes all friction. They say yes even if mildly interested.
"Pehla mahina zero. Install karo, use karo, pasand na aaye toh cancel — koi cost nahi, koi contract nahi." Conversion: 3x higher vs paid-from-day-1.
Reciprocity Setup
Free audit pehle, pitch baad mein
Local owners feel obligated if you help them first. Small free help = big yes chance.
"Bhaiya, main aapke Zomato page ka free review karunga — kya improve kar sakte hain. Phir ye QR dekh sakte ho agar interest ho." Free help first = warm conversation.
Same Day Close
Aaj nahi toh kabhi nahi
Local owner "sochenge" ka matlab hai forget karna. Same visit mein close karo — ya probability 80% girta hai.
"Kal install karna hai toh number de do abhi — form fill karta hoon yahan pe." Pen nikalo, form dikhao. Physical action = commitment trigger.
Specific Proof
Vague claim vs specific number
Local owners don't believe marketing language. Specific numbers from specific businesses = real proof.
"[Specific dhaba name], [area] — August mein 247 QR scans, 89 WhatsApp orders directly, ₹23,400 Zomato commission bachaya." Numbers + name = credibility.

Client Behavior — 8 Types

Type 1 — Restaurant Owner
The Zomato Feeder
Roz Zomato commission jaata hai — dard real hai lekin numb ho gaya hai. Trigger: exact monthly loss calculate karke dikhao live. Calculator nikaalo, unka daily order count multiply karo. "Ye paise aapke the." Closes fast once they SEE the number.
Type 2 — Kirana Owner
The Trust-First Buyer
Technology se darta hai. Paas ki dukaan ka reference chahiye. "Aapke customer QR scan karenge kya?" Trigger: neighbor proof + free trial. "Ek mahina free try karo — apne customers se khud pucho." Slow yes lekin once yes = never cancels.
Type 3 — Salon Owner
The Relationship Seeker
Appointment booking ke dard se connect karo — "Kitni baar customers aati hain then disappear?" QR appointment + reminder solves this. Personal connection strong honi chahiye — ye emotional buyer hai logical nahi. Didi/madam se ache se baat karo.
Type 4 — Medical/Clinic
The Evidence Demander
Data aur proof chahiye. "Kitne patients ne use kiya?" type questions aayenge. Case study + percentage improvement stats ready rakkho. Don't oversell — underpromise. "Ye minimum 15% appointment increase karta hai — typically zyada hota hai." Conservative claims = more credibility.
Type 5 — Hotel/PG Owner
The Math Calculator
OTA commission number sunne ke baad calculator nikalta hai. Let them calculate themselves — don't tell, ask: "Ek mahine mein OYO se kitni bookings? [X] × ₹400 commission = [calculated]. Ye aapka savings potential hai." Their own math = strongest close.
Type 6 — Street Food/Small Vendor
The Cash-Only Skeptic
UPI se payment bhi doubt karta hai. Free trial offer zaroori — "Paisa mat do abhi." Monthly rather than annual. Referral incentive works well. Usually 3-4 touchpoints lagte hain. But volume play — bahut zyada vendors hain. Low ticket ₹999/month ke.
Type 7 — Multi-Chain Owner (2+ branches)
The ROI Maximizer
Already business-minded. Fastest close if numbers check out. Premium plan push karo. Annual payment prefer karte hain. Ask immediately: "Doosri branch hai? Wahan bhi install karein — 20% off." Multi-location = 2-3x revenue from one relationship.
Type 8 — Existing Website Client
The Easy Upsell
Trust already hai. QR add-on easy sell. "Aapka website toh hai — ye QR ussi se connect hota hai. Extra ₹2K mein physical world mein bhi digital loyalty ho jaata hai." Close rate 70%+ for existing clients. Always pitch QR when doing website projects.

Installation SOP — Har Client Ke Liye
1
Day 1 — Information Gather: Business name, menu items (20-30 max), owner photo, WhatsApp number, preferred loyalty reward (free item ya discount).
2
Day 2 — Setup (2 hours): Digital menu create karo, QR generate karo, WhatsApp integration test karo, loyalty system setup karo, analytics tracking add karo.
3
Day 3 — Install karo physically: QR print karo (A5 laminated ₹30-50). Counter pe, table pe, door pe lagao. 3-5 spots minimum per location.
4
Day 3 — Training (30 min): Owner + 1 staff ko sikhao — kaise check kare orders, kaise loyalty points redeem kare, kaise analytics dekhe.
5
Week 2 — Check-in call: Scan ho raha hai? Orders aa rahe hain? Koi problem? Proactively call karo — don't wait for complaint.
6
Month 1 end — Results report bhejo: Total scans, orders, loyalty points collected, estimated savings. Isi report se paid conversion easy hoti hai.
QR Print Specifications
A5
Standard table/counter standee. Laminated. Cost: ₹30-40 each. Minimum 3 per location.
A4
Window/door display. Bigger visibility. "Scan for menu & loyalty rewards" text include karo.
Sticker
3×3 inch sticker — POS counter, delivery bags, packaging. Cost: ₹5-10 each. 20+ per location.
Tent
Table tent card (triangle fold) — restaurant tables. Professional look. Cost: ₹15-20 each.
Printing tip: Bulk print sab clients ka ek saath — per unit cost ₹10-15 kam hogi. 50 clients × 5 QRs × ₹30 = ₹7,500 printing cost. Charge ₹500 as "installation fee" = printing covered.

Best Case Scenarios
🚀
Ek market mein word spreads — 10 referrals ek din mein
BEST▼

Kya karo immediately: Turat un 10 referrals ke paas personally jao — same day. "Sunao, [aapke reference name] ne bata ke bheja. Unke yahan chal raha hai, aapke liye bhi 30 min mein ready kar deta hoon — free pehla mahina." Conversion 80%+ when warm referral.

🏢
Restaurant chain (10+ outlets) approach kare
BEST▼

Corporate deal: ₹1,500/outlet/month × 10 outlets = ₹15,000/month from one client. Centralized dashboard, franchise-level analytics, dedicated support. Meeting: CEO/owner level, custom proposal, 1-week timeline. Agreement carefully banao. Pehle 50% advance ₹7,500.

Worst Case Scenarios + Solutions
❌
Client cancel kar raha hai after 3 months
CRITICAL▼

Root cause first: Kyun cancel kar raha hai? 3 reasons hote hain: 1) Results nahi aaye, 2) Paise tight hain, 3) Competitor ne approach kiya.

If results nahi aaye: "Ek month extension free deta hoon — aur main personally scan rate improve karne ki strategy deta hoon. Kya try karein?" Usually stays.

If paise tight hain: "₹999 wale starter plan pe le aata hoon temporarily. Jab business accha hoga, upgrade karenge." Half revenue better than zero.

If competitor: "Kya feature unka zyada accha hai? Main wahi feature add karta hoon — ya agar better deal hai toh sach mein jaao, main nahin rukne dunga." Honesty = sometimes they stay.
💸
30 clients ne ek saath renewal nahi kiya
WORST▼

Agar 30 clients = ₹60K/month risk hai — ye kabhi ek saath nahi hona chahiye agar properly onboard kiya ho.

Prevention: Annual contracts push karo — sirf monthly clients = churn risk. Annual client ne advance de diya = committed.

If happens: Win-back campaign — personal call har client ko, updated results dikhao, 15% renewal discount offer karo (sirf renewal ke liye, not new). Target: 50% win-back = 15 clients back = ₹30K recovered.
📱
System technically fail ho gaya — QR kaam nahi kar raha
CRITICAL▼
Prevention: Har QR URL ke liye backup URL rakho. Domain renewal auto-pay set karo. Monthly server uptime check karo.

If happens: 2-hour fix rule. Client ko 15 min mein notify karo: "Technical issue detect hua — 2 hours mein fix hoga. Inconvenience ke liye sorry — ye month ka ek week extra milega." Then actually fix it. Never go silent.
😤
Client complain karta hai customers scan nahi kar rahe
COMMON▼

Ye most common complaint hai — usually client ki problem hai, QR ki nahi. QR placement galat ya staff promote nahi kar raha hota hai.

Solution: Site visit karo personally.
Check: QR placement eye-level hai? Lighting acchi hai? Staff customers ko bata raha hai?
Add: "Scan karo 10% off" ka sticker QR ke upar. This alone = 3-4x scan rate.
Training: Staff ko sikhao ki har customer ko physically batayein — "QR scan karo, loyalty points milenge."
🏃
Field agent ne chhod diya + clients unsettle ho gaye
RISK▼
Prevention: Agent ke clients ka data tumhare CRM mein — not just agent's phone. Har client ke saath direct WhatsApp connection tumhara bhi hona chahiye.

If happens: Personally call all affected clients within 24hrs. "Main directly handle kar raha hoon aapka account — koi gap nahi ayega." Ek week mein replacement hire karo. Har client ke liye 1 month extra free as goodwill.

Master Rules — QR Business ke liye

Ye tod ke clients ja chuke hain — ye follow karo aur stable raho

Non-Negotiable Rules
R-01
Annual contract push karo hamesha. Monthly = client can leave anytime. Annual = guaranteed revenue. Even ₹15K annual better than ₹2K × 12 monthly (because monthly churn happens).
R-02
QR kaam karta rahe 24/7 — downtime = cancellation trigger. Server reliable rakho. Domain auto-renew. Monthly backup. SLA: 99% uptime.
R-03
Month 1 results report bhejo har client ko. Results dikhte hain toh renewal automatic hai. Results nahi dikhaye toh client khud cancel karne ka reason dhundhta hai.
R-04
Client data sirf client ka. Ek restaurant ke customer data doosre ko mat do. Trust breach = immediate cancellation + reputation damage.
R-05
Installation ke 3 din baad check-in call zaroori. Problems early mein pakad lo — "Kaise chal raha hai?" — yahi relationship banata hai.
R-06
Agent ke saath client ka direct number tumhare paas bhi. Agent gaya toh client retain karna easy hoga agar relationship tumhari hai bhi.
R-07
Free month ke baad renewal conversation 5 days pehle start karo. Day 25 pe: results bhejo. Day 28: "Agle mahine ke liye payment link." Day 30: last reminder. Never wait for client to realize month end hua.
R-08
Referral program har client ko bata do installation pe hi. "Ek referral = 1 month free." Agar nahi bataya pehle din toh yaad nahi rahega.
Rule Break Recovery
B-01
System down raha 6+ hours: Personal apology call + 1 week free compensation. Baad mein: reliable hosting upgrade karo. Repetition = client gone.
B-02
Client ka renewal miss hua aur cancel kar diya: Win-back call within 24 hrs. Updated results + 15% discount for re-signup. 40-50% clients wapas aate hain if approached within 1 week.
B-03
Agent ne fake installs dikhaye (dishonest reporting): Immediate termination. Client ko personally call karo, truth batao, free month compensation do. Better to lose agent than client + reputation.

Client Retention System

Repeat revenue ka matlab hai churn zero rakhna — ye exactly kaise karo

Retention = Real Business. New client acquire karna 5x costly hai retain karne se. Tumhara ₹1L/month business tabhi stable hai jab 90%+ clients hargiz renew karein. Iske liye ye system follow karo exactly.

Monthly Retention SOP — Har Client
W1
Week 1 after install: Check-in WhatsApp — "Kaise chal raha hai? Scan ho rahe hain?" Personal message, not broadcast. Shows you care.
W2
Week 2: First scan report share karo — "Ye haftebhar ke scans hain. Ye progress hai." Even if small numbers — share. Transparency builds trust.
M1
Month 1 end: Full results report PDF/image — scans, orders, loyalty customers, estimated savings. "Ye free month mein hua — paid mein aur feature milenge." Conversion trigger.
M2
Month 2+: Monthly brief update WhatsApp (every month 25th) — this month ki performance vs last month. Growing = client stays happy. Declining = you address proactively.
Q1
Quarterly review call (3 months): 15-min call — "Kya improve karein?" Ask for feedback. Implement at least 1 suggestion. Client feel karta hai valued. Upsell opportunity here.
Churn Warning Signals — Immediately Act Karo
⚠
Client ne 2+ weeks se WhatsApp pe reply nahi kiya — call karo personally. Ghost hona = planning to cancel.
⚠
Client ke QR scans 50%+ drop ho gaye — site visit karo. Placement change hua? Staff not promoting?
⚠
Client ne complaint ki — resolve within 2 hours. Unresolved complaint = guaranteed cancellation.
⚠
Renewal date 10 din door hai aur client ne confirm nahi kiya — proactively reach out with results + payment link.
Referral Engine — Har Client Se 1-2 New Clients
1
Installation pe hi batao: "Ek referral de do — aapka ek month free." Specifically unse 2-3 naam maango — "Paas ki kaunsi dukaan hai jo ye use kar sakti hai?"
2
Month 1 results report ke saath: "Agar results se khush ho — kisi ek ko bata do mere baare mein." Easy ask after delivering value.
3
Happy client ke yahan physically jao — "Bhaiya, apne mohalle mein kaun hai jise ye chahiye?" In-person referral request = 3x more effective than WhatsApp request.
4
Champion client identify karo (wo jo khud deta hai referral) — these people deserve extra care. Free upgrade, priority support, personal relationship. They'll keep sending clients for years.
