Standard Operating Procedures

Har kaam ka fixed process — founder bhi yahi, team bhi yahi

SOP-001 — New Lead (Golden 1-Hour Rule)
Lead aaya (email/call/LinkedIn/Instagram/referral) → CRM mein turat enter karo
CRM columns: Name, Business, Phone, Email, Niche, Source, Stage, Last Contact, Notes
Niche identify → relevant persona ka email use karo (medical=Rahul, hotel=Amit, school=Priya)
Within 1 HOUR: Personalized email bhejo with demo link. 1 ghante baad response rate 80% girta hai.
24hrs no reply → WhatsApp follow-up: "Kya email mili? Ek quick cheez dikhani thi."
48hrs no reply → Phone call (Sales Manager kare, calling script use karo)
Demo schedule → Google Meet link + Calendar invite dono bhejo
Demo ke 2 hours baad → Proposal email with 3 pricing options + ROI calculator
SOP-002 — Demo Call (15-Minute Formula)
Call se 10 min pehle: client ka business Google karo, Instagram dekho, problem confirm karo
Min 0-3: "Aapka [business] kitne saal se chal raha hai?" — rapport, unhe bolne do
Min 3-5: "Ek cheez puchni thi — [pain point] aapke saath bhi hota hai?" — unka dard unhi ke mooh se
Min 5-12: Demo — sirf unki problem ka solution. Features mat ginao. "Dekho, ye hota hai aapka problem solve."
Min 12-15: ROI calculation live — "Loss tha X, system cost Y, ROI 8x." Numbers dikhao.
Always end: "Kya aap kal se trial shuru karenge?" — always ask for commitment, never end without CTA
SOP-003 — Deal Close Karna
Proposal: 3 tiers — Basic ₹12K / Standard ₹25K (Most Popular) / Premium ₹60K
Urgency: "Ye offer sirf Friday tak hai" (genuine reason: bandwidth, team capacity)
50% advance lene ke BAAD kaam shuru karo — no exception, ever
Agreement via WhatsApp bhi legally valid — "Scope: X, Amount: Y, Timeline: Z, Advance: 50% — Agree?" + their "Haan" = contract
UPI + Bank transfer dono options do. Never refuse any payment method.
Delivery ke 48hrs baad: testimonial + Google review maango — script ready hai
SOP-004 — Virtual Team Daily Routine
9:00 AM — WhatsApp standup (5 lines text): "Kal kya hua + aaj kya karunga" — everyone
9:30-11 — Lead hunting: LinkedIn search + Google Maps + IndiaMart browse
11:00-1 — Cold outreach: 50 emails + 20 LinkedIn DMs per person
2:00-4 — Follow-ups, demo calls, proposal sends
4:00-5 — CRM update mandatory. Sales Manager verify kare sabka CRM is updated.
6:00 PM — 1 social media post per persona (batch create Sunday ko for the week)
SOP-005 — Client Onboarding After Deal
Payment ke 1 hour mein: Welcome email — confirmation + timeline + what to expect + emergency contact
WhatsApp group banao: Client + Sales Manager + Developer (3 people) — single communication channel
Day 1-3: Requirements form bhejo (Google Form) ya 20-min discovery call
Day 7: Progress screenshot bhejo — even if partial. "Updates are good news."
Delivery day: Live demo call karo — walk them through their product personally
Day 30 after delivery: Check-in call — kya sab theek hai? Upsell opportunity here.

Sales Psychology

Kya trigger karta hai brain ko "haan" bolne ke liye — Nobel Prize-backed science

Loss Aversion
Nuksaan batao, fayda nahi
Kahneman ka proof: humans loss se 2.5x zyada darते hain gain se.

Wrong: "20% revenue increase hoga"

Right: "₹45,000/month ja raha hai no-show se. Kitna aur afford karenge?"

Social Proof
Unhi jaisi company ka naam lo
People follow people LIKE them, not better than them. Same city = max trust.

"[Same-area clinic] jo bilkul aapke paas hai — last month humse sign kiya. Same problem tha." Fir show reference.

Scarcity + Urgency
Real urgency banana — fake mat karo
Fake scarcity backfires. Real reasons create genuine urgency.

"Rajasthan mein sirf 5 medical clients is quarter — 2 slots baki hain. Dedicated support deni padti hai har client ko."

Reciprocity
Pehle dena, phir maangna
Cialdini principle #1 — free value creates psychological debt.

"Maine aapki clinic ka WhatsApp audit kiya — 3 gaps hain jo revenue miss kar rahi hain. Bata sakta hoon free mein?"

Authority
Expert banke aao, vendor nahi
5 min research before call. Competitor mention in meeting = instant authority.

"Healthcare software mein 8 saal ka experience hai — Jaipur ke 20+ clinics ke saath kaam kiya. Ye pattern hamesha dekhta hoon."

Commitment Ladder
Chhote "haan" se bade "haan" tak
Har micro-commitment ke baad close easier hota hai.

Q1: "No-show problem hai?" Q2: "Solution chahiye?" Q3: "₹25K sahi hai ₹45K/month bachaane ke liye?" → Close.

Client Behavior Patterns

8 types of clients — kaise sochte hain, kaise respond karte hain, kaise handle karo

Type 1 — Doctor / Medical Owner
The Busy Skeptic
Pehla call ignore karta hai. Demo mein 5 sharp questions karta hai. Reference check karta hai. Ek baar satisfied hua toh 3-5 referrals khud deta hai. Don't push — create urgency through numbers only.
Type 2 — School / Coaching Owner
The Committee Seeker
"Staff se poochna hai" — committee decision. 2-3 follow-ups lagenge. Jan-Mar (admission season) mein most receptive. Demo pe accountant bhi aa sakta hai — use address karo directly.
Type 3 — Hotel / Restaurant Owner
The ROI Calculator
Seedha puchta hai: "Kitna badhega revenue?" Numbers dikhao toh fast decide karta hai. Off-season mein approach karo — peak mein busy hota hai, nahi sunta.
Type 4 — Real Estate Builder
The Power Player
Bade naam pe react karta hai. References zaroori. First meeting = relationship only, no pitch. High value deal, longest cycle 2-4 months. Lunch ya site visit invite karo — closes happen in person.
Type 5 — Salon / Gym Owner
The Impulsive Buyer
Demo accha laga toh wahi pe decide karta hai. Small ticket fast close. Problem: payment delay zyada. ALWAYS 50% advance — no exceptions with this type especially.
Type 6 — The Ghost (No Reply)
Interested But Stuck
Demo dekha, interested laga — phir silence. Reason: budget approval, busy, ya competitor compare kar raha hai. Solution: 6-touchpoint follow-up sequence. Day 30 "budget change hua?" message works 40% of time.
Type 7 — The Price Fighter
Always Negotiating
Har cheez mein discount. Never give more than 10% without getting something back. Script: "Agar aaj confirm karo toh 10% off + 3 months support — ye mera best possible offer hai." Package discount, not just price cut.
Type 8 — Champion Client
The Referral Machine
Khush hua toh sabko batata hai. Doctors especially — unka professional network tight hota hai. 1 satisfied doctor = 3-5 referrals. Incentive: "Ek referral = 1 month free support." This type builds your business free.

Worst Case Scenarios + Step-by-Step Solutions
😰
90 days — zero clients. Kya karu?
WORST▼

Pehle 45-60 days tak ye hona normal hai. Panic mat karo.

Immediate actions:
1. Volume 5x karo — 50 emails/day se 250/day
2. Demo link seedha WhatsApp pe bhejo bina intro — "Ye dekho, aapke liye relevant hai kya?"
3. Free audit offer: "Aapki clinic ka free digital audit karunga — koi commitment nahi"

5. Existing network: Pehle apne 500 contacts mein se 50 ko personally WhatsApp karo
6. Price experiment: ₹5-8K ka starter package introduce karo — foot in the door

💸
Client ne payment nahi ki — deal close ke baad
WORST▼

50% advance rule toot gayi toh ye ZAROOR hoga kisi point pe.

Prevention: 50% advance rule — NEVER break. No exceptions.
If it happens anyway:
D1: Friendly WhatsApp — "Invoice pending thi, koi issue?"
D3: Formal email with invoice attached again
D7: "Quality kaam ke liye payment process karna padega — kaam rok raha hoon tab tak"
D14: Legal notice via email (simple letter format, no lawyer needed first time)
D21: Consumer forum complaint (if ₹25K+)
Never lose temper publicly — industry word spreads fast.

🔥
Client unhappy — bad Google review dena chahta hai
CRITICAL▼

2-hour response rule — always. "Main personally dekh raha hoon — 24 hours mein resolve karunga."

If review already posted:
→ Professional public response: "Issue X resolve kar diya gaya [Date] pe. Client satisfaction hamare liye priority hai."
→ Specific fix karo unki problem
→ Extra: 1 month free support do — cost ₹0, goodwill high
→ 5 new reviews collect karo happy clients se — dilute the effect

Future readers will judge your response more than the review itself.

👥
Virtual persona expose — "Ye real person hai?"
RISK▼

Koi client reverse-image search kare ya LinkedIn cross-check kare.

Prevention: AI photo (not stolen), dedicated SIM, consistent backstory notes, real testimonials under persona name.

If caught: Honest, calm: "Ye hamare specialist consultant hain — niche-specific experts. Unka kaam real hai, results real hain." Redirect to results: "Demo mein jo solution dekha — ye ab bhi same hai. Results ke baare mein baat karein?"

Most clients care about results, not org structure.

📉
Competitor zyada sasta de raha hai — "Wo ₹8K mein de raha hai"
COMMON▼

Never compete on price. Script: "₹8K wala system kyun ₹8K mein hai — specifically 3 cheezein hain jo unke paas nahi hain:"
1. Niche-specific features (clinic-specific vs generic)
2. After-sale support (after ₹8K, koi nahi milta)
3. Local Jaipur-based team (24hr support, site visit possible)

Then: "Ek client mere paas 3 months baad aaya — pehle cheap system liya tha, support zero tha. Tabse hamare saath hai." Real story always closes.

🔄
Client unlimited changes maang raha hai after delivery
VERY COMMON▼

Prevention: Agreement mein likho: "3 revision rounds included. Additional changes: ₹X per change."
Pehle client ka requirements WRITTEN format mein lo — Google Form ya email. Unka approval lo before starting.

If happening now: "Ye scope se bahar ja raha hai — isme ₹X additional lagega, 2 din mein complete. Chhote changes (under 30 min) free kar deta hoon — bade ke liye quote dunga." Firm lekin polite.

🏃
Key team member suddenly exit kar gaya
RISK▼

Prevention: Har role ke liye hamesha 1-2 backup candidates shortlisted rakho. Sab processes documented SOP mein — ek banda jaaye, SOP se doosra uthaye kaam.

If it happens: 48 hours mein replacement hire karo. Commission structure attractive rakho. Exit interview karo — understand karo reason. Team morale ke liye remaining team ko immediately reassure karo. Client communication: "Team update hua hai — aapka kaam unaffected hai."

More Common Situations
⏰
Delivery deadline miss hone wali hai — client ko kab batao?
COMMON▼

Rule: Proactive communication always beats reactive. Deadline se 2-3 din pehle call karo: "Update dena chahta tha — [specific reason] ke wajah se 2 din extra lag rahe hain. [Date] tak confirm milega." Client angry nahi hota — relieved hota hai ke informed hai. Silent rehke miss karna = trust permanently broken.

💬
Sales Manager ke targets consistently miss ho rahe hain
COMMON▼

First 30 days grace period. After that: 1-on-1 review — specific metrics check karo (emails/day, calls/day, demos/week). Problem usually: wrong lead targeting, poor follow-up, or weak demo skills. Solve with: roleplay training + better scripts. If still no improvement after 60 days — replace. Commission-only hire = low switching cost for both parties.

Master Rules — Jo Kabhi Nahi Toodne

Ye rules toot jaayein toh business toot jaata hai

Non-Negotiable Rules (NEVER BREAK — EVER)
R-01
50% advance before work starts. No exceptions. No "kal de dunga." No "trust karo." 50% advance ya kaam shuru nahi. Ek baar rule toda toh team bhi todegi — culture set hoti hai top se.
R-02
Written agreement har deal pe. WhatsApp message legally valid — "Scope: X, Amount: Y, Timeline: Z, Advance: 50% — Agree?" + their "Haan" = contract India mein. Screenshot save karo har deal ka.
R-03
Delivery timeline always underpromise. "10 days lagenge" bolke 7 mein do = wow. "5 days" bolke 10 mein do = lost client + bad review. One missed deadline = all referrals gone permanently.
R-04
Persona cross-contamination — kabhi nahi. Rahul sirf medical. Amit sirf hospitality. Agar Rahul kisi hotel client se baat kare toh whole persona network credibility crash ho sakti hai.
R-05
Competitor badnaam mat karo publicly. "Hum different hain" = okay. "Wo bekar hain" = reputation damage aapka. Industry chhoti hoti hai — log jaante hain ek doosre ko.
R-06
Client data share nahi. Client A ka data Client B ko kabhi mat batao — even as example. Trust = business ki real currency. One breach = both clients gone + industry reputation damage.
R-07
Har complaint ka 2-hour response. Even "Main dekh raha hoon — 4 ghante mein update dunga" = professional. Ignore = review bomb guaranteed. Response time = trust signal to all future clients also.
R-08
Team commission — 7 days mein paid. Late commission = team motivation zero = exit. Fast payment = loyalty + hustle. Team ko pata hona chahiye ki earn kiya toh milega turat.
R-09
Kisi bhi client ko publicly embarrass mat karo. Private mein sab kuch — complaints, disputes, negotiations. Public disagreements destroy both parties' reputation.
R-10
Har deal ke baad testimonial/review maango. Within 48 hours of delivery. Strike when happiness is fresh. "Aapka ek review mujhe bahut help karega." Simple ask. Huge long-term value.
Strong Guidelines (Rarely Break, With Good Reason Only)
G-01
Ek time pe ek niche focus. Exception: Agar referral hi doosri niche se aaye — never say no to a warm referral, regardless of niche.
G-02
Demo free hoti hai. Exception: ₹5L+ enterprise deal ke liye paid discovery ₹3-5K charge kar sakte ho — adjust in final price if they sign.
G-03
10% se zyada discount nahi. Exception: Bundle deal (3 products together) ya multi-year retainer contract sign kare.
G-04
150 cold emails/day per email ID max. Exception: Short-term campaign 200 tak okay — 2 weeks rest mandatory after.
Rule Break Recovery — Master Plan
B1
Advance liye bina kaam kiya: Kaam rok do immediately. Politely: "Quality ensure karne ke liye advance policy hai — ye ek firm rule hai hamare saath." Client serious hai toh advance dega. Nahi diya = not serious = saved your time.
B2
Deadline miss ho gayi: Khud call karo BEFORE deadline — proactive. "Ek din late ho raha hai — reason X. [New date] tak confirmed." Silence = anger. Proactive = forgiveness 90% of time.
B3
Persona expose ho gayi: Calm, honest explanation ready — "Specialist consultants hain, results real hain." Redirect to results + references. Most clients care about delivery, not internal org structure.
B4
Bad review aa gayi publicly: 2-hour professional response. Fix the issue. Extra free support. Collect 5 new reviews from happy clients to dilute. Never argue publicly.
B5
Team member ne data leak kiya: Immediately access revoke. Client inform karo honestly. Assess damage. Legal notice to ex-employee if warranted. Tighten all access controls. Make all future hires sign NDA.
B6
Competitor ne copied your system/website: First: Don't panic. Document with screenshots + date. If substantial copying: DMCA notice. Legal action only if ₹5L+ damage. Better response: Move faster, innovate, make copying irrelevant.

Security & Protection

Virtual identity, client data, financial security — sab protect karo

Virtual Persona Security
• Photo: AI-generated (thispersondoesnotexist.com type) — real kisi ka photo mat use karo
• LinkedIn: Alag browser per account. Mobile data for each if 5+ accounts same IP
• Cross-link: Personas ek dusre ko publicly connect mat karo on LinkedIn
• Phone: Dedicated SIM per persona ya 2ndLine virtual number app
• Consistency: Persona notes: city, company, years exp, education — sab consistent rakho. Inconsistency = exposure.
Client Data Security
• Client credentials — encrypted doc mein. Never in plain text email/WhatsApp
• Database: Firebase/AWS — encrypted at rest by default
• Agreement mein: "Aapka data sirf aapke project ke liye, no third-party sharing"
• Password manager: Bitwarden (free) — master password only founder knows
• 2FA: All company accounts mandatory (Gmail, LinkedIn, hosting, CRM, Razorpay)
• Offboarding: Jab team member leave kare — same day, all access revoke karo
Website Security
• SSL — always https. Hosting se Let's Encrypt free milta hai
• Admin URL change: /wp-admin → /vstechadmin2025 (WordPress)
• Strong admin password — 16+ chars, password manager mein
• Wordfence plugin — free version kafi for WordPress protection
• Weekly automated backups — UpdraftPlus plugin
• Demo pages: Rate limiting — 10+ requests/min from same IP block karo
• Contact form: reCAPTCHA v3 lagao — spam zero ho jaata hai
Financial Security
• GST registered — sab transactions official. Vyapar app — free invoice generator
• Separate business account — personal + business never mix karo
• UPI + bank transfer + cash — sab options do
• Save 30% of every payment for taxes (advance tax quarterly)
• Agreement: Payment dispute = Jaipur arbitration jurisdiction
• Every deal: UPI screenshot + email confirmation — evidence ke liye save karo

Security Mindset: Security ka matlab paranoia nahi — preparation hai. Agar aaj sab systems fail ho jaayein: client data safe hai? Team ko access hai zaroori cheezein ka? Business 48 hours mein recover ho sakta hai? Agar haan — security strong hai.
