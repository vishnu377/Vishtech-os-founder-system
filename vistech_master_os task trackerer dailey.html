    MASTER STRATEGIES

    Sirf plan banana kafi nahi. Ye rules roz follow karo.

    THE NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES

    • 01
      Morning Anchor — First 2 Hours Sacred HainSubah uthh ke pehle 2 ghante sirf deep work mein. Phone band. Social media nahi. Ye 2 ghante hi teri empire ke foundation hain. Jo bhi "most important" kaam hai — woh pehle 2 ghante mein karo.
    • 02
      10-Client Rule — Roz 10 Naye ContactsChahe website ho, QR system ho, kuch bhi — roz 10 naye log/businesses ko reach karo. 10 rejection bhi theek hai. 10 mein se 1 bhi haan bole to din successful hai.
    • 03
      90-Day Trial Rule — Kisi par bhi jaldi trust nahiKoi bhi partner, team member, client — pehle 90 din observe karo. Performance dekho, promise vs delivery dekho. 90 din ke baad decide karo trust karna hai ya nahi.
    • 04
      24-Hour Rule — Emotional decisions nahiGusse mein, dukh mein, ya bahut zyada khushi mein koi bhi bada decision mat lo. 24 ghante ruko. Fir soche. Ye ek rule thousands of bad decisions se bachata hai.
    • 05
      Weekly Review — Saturday 1 HourHar Saturday: Is hafte kya kiya? Income kitni aayi? Clients kitne mile? Agla hafte ka plan kya hai? Bina review ke growth nahi hoti — tujhe pata hi nahi chalega kahan jaa raha hai.
    • 06
      Skill-First SpendingPehle jo bhi extra paise aayein — business skills pe lagao. Tools, courses, software. Entertainment, gadgets, clothes — baad mein. Har rupee jo tu khud mein lagata hai 10x return deta hai.
    • 07
      Document EverythingHar client conversation screenshot lo. Har deal writing mein karo. Har payment receipt rakho. Business mein memory pe nahi, documents pe chalte hain.

    BATCH PROCESSING
    Ek kaam ek hi waqt pe karo. Aaj Instagram posts bana rahe ho? 7 din ke posts ek hi session mein banao. Roz alag karne mein 5x zyada time lagta hai setup-teardown mein.

    ENERGY MANAGEMENT
    High energy = creative/sales work. Low energy = routine/admin work. Subah uthh ke subse pehle call mat karo — body warm-up chahiye. Peak hours: 9 AM–1 PM typically.

    TEAM MEMBER EVALUATION CHECKLIST

    Kisi ko bhi hire karo ya partner banao — ye 5 cheezein dekho:

    1. TRACK RECORD — Pehle kya kiya hai? Proof hai? References check kiye?
    2. COMMUNICATION — Ye log reply fast karte hain? Clear bolte hain? Ya vague rehte hain?
    3. MONEY ATTITUDE — Pehle hi bahut zyada maang rahe hain bina prove kiye? Red flag.
    4. DEADLINE RESPECT — Pehle task do, chhota kaam do — dekho deliver karte hain ya excuses dete hain
    5. REACTION TO FEEDBACK — Criticism sunke kya karte hain? Defensive? Ya improve karte hain?

    EMOTIONAL DISCIPLINE

    Ye section sabse important hai. Intelligence se zyada emotional control determine karta hai who succeeds. Roz padho.

    EMOTIONS ARE DATA · NOT DIRECTIONS
    🧊
    THE 24-HOUR RULE
    Koi bhi emotion feel ho — gussa, dard, excitement, fear — 24 ghante koi bada decision nahi. Soo jao, kal soche. 80% "urgent" problems khud solve ho jaati hain.
    RULE: Agar aaj decide karna bahut zaroori lag raha hai — it's not. Wait.
    🎯
    COMPARISON IS POISON
    Accenture wala, GF ka naya partner, dost ki job — ye sab alag journey hain. Tu apne 2025 version se compare kar apne aap ko. Baaki sab irrelevant hain. Unka path tera path nahi hai.
    RULE: Roz ek cheez likho — "aaj main pehle se better hun kyunki..."
    🔇
    VALIDATION SEEKERS FAIL
    Kisi ko prove karne ki zaroorat nahi. Na family ko, na ex ko, na dosto ko. Tu jab unhe prove karna band karega — tab teri asli power shuru hogi. Apne liye karo. Sirf apne liye.
    RULE: "Main ye unhe dikhane ke liye kar raha hun?" — agar haan, ruk. Rethink.
    💬
    EMOTIONAL PEOPLE SE BUSINESS NAHI
    Agar koi bahut jaldi aggressive hota hai, bahut jaldi blame karta hai, bahut jaldi expectations change karta hai — ye client ya partner nahi lena. Emotional turbulence = business problem guaranteed.
    RULE: Gut feeling sun. Agar uncomfortable lag raha hai meeting mein — trust the feeling.
    🔋
    ENERGY MANAGEMENT = MONEY MANAGEMENT
    Negative log, useless arguments, social media drama, overthinking — ye sab tere energy bankrupt kar dete hain. Har cheez jo tujhe drain karti hai — remove karo. Ye self-care nahi, ye survival hai.
    RULE: Raat ko socho — "kya aaj meri energy sahi jagah gayi?" Agar nahi — kal badlo.
    🌊
    REJECTION IS REDIRECTION
    Client ne nahi kaha, job nahi mili, deal fail hui — ye failure nahi. Ye data hai. Kya sikhna hai? Kya alag karna hai? Har "nahi" tujhe sahi direction mein le jaata hai agar tu seekhne ki attitude rakhe.
    RULE: Har rejection ke baad: "Main isse kya seekh sakta hun?" — likho.
    📵
    DIGITAL DETOX — DAILY
    Roz 1 ghanta phone band. Khaana khate waqt, subah ke pehle 30 min, raat ko soone se 30 min pehle. Social media scroll karna tere competitors ki intelligence nahi badhata — sirf tera time waste karta hai.
    RULE: Phone bedroom mein mat rakho. Alarm ghadi le lo. Seriously.
